Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Last Tram Café is made by Petr Vnenk. This policy covers the game on Steam, the App Store and Google Play.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send any data over the internet.

What stays on your device

Your progress (shift number, money earned, décor you have bought and the regulars' stories you have seen) is saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device, and deleting the game deletes it.

Store platforms

Steam, Apple and Google may collect information when you buy or download games through their stores. That is covered by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
