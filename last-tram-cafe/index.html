Support

Last Tram Café is a small, calm café sim. Brew coffee by hand, get to know the regulars and watch the rain and the trams go by.

Contact
For help, bug reports or feedback, email petr.vnenk@gmail.com. Please mention your platform (Steam, iPhone/iPad or Android) and what happened.

Saving

Progress is saved automatically on your device at the end of each shift.

Controls

Click a cup to carry it, or drag it where it should go. Right-click puts it back. P switches between serving and watching, B walks around the café, M toggles music, H hides the interface, Esc pauses.
